Hum

Machines tell you before they break. Point a microphone at one and listen.

1

Put the device near a running machine

A fridge, fan, pump, washing machine, laptop under load.
2

Hum listens for 45 seconds to learn what healthy sounds like

It builds a statistical fingerprint of that machine, in that room.
3

Then it watches for the sound drifting away from that fingerprint

And tells you which frequencies moved, not just that something changed.

Audio never leaves this device. There is no server, no upload, and no account — the analysis runs entirely in this tab, which is also why it works offline.

45

Learning what healthy sounds like

Keep the machine running normally and stay quiet.

Longer is better — the fingerprint has to cover the machine's whole normal cycle. A fridge that hasn't switched its compressor on yet will look anomalous the moment it does.
